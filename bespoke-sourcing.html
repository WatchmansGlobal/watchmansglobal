Our Practice

For watches that are not in any window.

The watches most worth owning rarely advertise themselves. They move between collectors quietly, often without ever appearing on a public listing. Our practice is built around those conversations.

You tell us the reference, the year, the dial; we work patiently and discreetly across our network until the right example surfaces. Authenticated before it reaches you. Insured to your door.

Authentication & Provenance

Every piece, verified.

No watch leaves our hands until its authenticity has been independently established.

Across pre-owned, vintage and grey-market acquisitions, authentication is the work that earns trust. We examine each piece in person and engage independent specialists where the reference or value warrants it.

Where a documented history exists it is shared with you. Where it does not, we say so plainly rather than imply it.

i.

Movement Inspection

Calibre, serial, finishing and operation independently verified against factory references for the period.

ii.

Case & Dial Examination

Original components confirmed; refinishing, service dials and luminous compound assessed with the appropriate magnification.

iii.

Documentation Verification

Warranty cards, service records, and receipts cross-checked against manufacturer and dealer records where available.

iv.

Service History

Records traced where possible; previous service work assessed for impact on originality.

v.

Market Comparison

Final pricing benchmarked against recent comparable sales so you know exactly where the piece sits in the market.

Discretion & Confidentiality

The work that does not happen on a screen.

Our network is built on years of relationships that exist precisely because they are not advertised. The pieces that move through us do so quietly — and your involvement does the same.

Your name never enters the market

Enquiries are made on our behalf, not yours. Sellers know there is a buyer for the piece; they do not know who.

No public listings

Pieces we hold in confidence are never published. The collection page reflects what is openly for sale; everything else stays in private channels.

Documentation to your standard

Invoices, customs paperwork and provenance records prepared in whichever name and form is most useful to you.

The conversation stays here

What is discussed in our enquiries remains between us. We never share client details or interests, in either direction.
